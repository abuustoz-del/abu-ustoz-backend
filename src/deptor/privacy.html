O'zbekcha · English

Deptor — kundalik xarajatlar, berilgan va olingan qarzlar hamda avans/to'lovlarni yozib borish va hafta, oy, yil yakunida hisobot olish uchun mobil ilova. Ushbu hujjat ilova ma'lumotlaringizga qanday munosabatda bo'lishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqasi: yozuvlaringiz qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Ixtiyoriy "Do'stlar bilan bog'lash" (Telegram orqali kirish) yoqilmasa, hech narsa serverga yuborilmaydi. Yoqsangiz, faqat telefon raqami ko'rsatilgan qarzlar va ularning qaytarishlari qarshi tomonga ko'rsatish uchun serverga yuboriladi. Reklama va kuzatuvchi analitika yo'q.

01 Qanday ma'lumotlar saqlanadi

Siz ilovaga kiritgan ma'lumotlar:

  • xarajatlar: summa, toifa, izoh va sana;
  • qarzlar va avans/to'lovlar: shaxsning ismi, ixtiyoriy telefon raqami, summa, sana, qaytarish muddati va izoh;
  • sozlamalar: til, valyuta, eslatma vaqti va SMS'da ko'rinadigan ismingiz.

Bu ma'lumotlar qurilmangiz ichidagi ma'lumotlar bazasida saqlanadi. Xarajatlar va kirimlar hech qachon serverga yuborilmaydi. Qarzlar faqat quyidagi ixtiyoriy bog'lash yoqilgan va qarzga telefon raqami kiritilgan bo'lsagina yuboriladi.

02 Do'stlar bilan bog'lash (ixtiyoriy)

Sozlamalarda Telegram orqali kirsangiz, qarzingiz qarshi tomonning Deptor ilovasida ham ko'rinadi (u tasdiqlaydi yoki rad etadi). Buning uchun:

  • Telegram'da botimizga o'z raqamingizni ulashasiz; bot tasdiqlangan telefon raqamingiz va Telegram ID raqamingizni oladi (kirishni tasdiqlash uchun).
  • Serverga yuboriladi: telefon raqamingiz, SMS'dagi ismingiz, telefon raqami kiritilgan qarzlar (qarshi tomon raqami, summa, valyuta, sana, muddat, izoh, yorlig'ingiz) va ularning qaytarishlari.
  • Qarshi tomon faqat shu qarz va uning qaytarishlarini, hamda sizning ismingiz va raqamingizni ko'radi. Boshqa yozuvlaringizni ko'rmaydi.
  • Ma'lumotlar faqat shu funksiya ishlashi uchun saqlanadi, sotilmaydi, reklamaga ishlatilmaydi.
  • Yozuvni o'chirsangiz, u serverda o'chirilgan deb belgilanadi va qarshi tomondan ham yo'qoladi. "Chiqish" bosilganda kirish bekor qilinadi. Serverdagi barcha ma'lumotlaringizni to'liq o'chirish uchun abuustoz@gmail.com ga yozing.
  • Aloqa shifrlangan (HTTPS) kanal orqali o'tadi.
  • Do'kon rejimi: do'kon yaratib sotuvchilarni qo'shsangiz, "do'kon qarzi" deb belgilangan yozuvlarni do'konning barcha a'zolari ko'radi va o'zgartira oladi. A'zo bo'lish uchun taklifni qabul qilish kerak. A'zo do'kondan chiqarilsa, u do'kon qarzlarini ko'rmay qoladi.
  • O'zgarishlar tarixi: serverda qarzlar bo'yicha kim va qachon nima qilgani (yozdi, tahrirladi, o'chirdi, qaytarish kiritdi) yozib boriladi. Tarixni qarzning yaratuvchi tomoni ko'ra oladi. O'chirilgan qarzlar serverda saqlanadi va ularni tiklash mumkin.
  • Rasmlar: qarzga rasm biriktirsangiz (masalan, qarz olgan odam, hujjat yoki dalil), u telefonda saqlanadi. Agar qarz boshqa odam yoki do'kon bilan bog'langan bo'lsa, rasm serverga ham yuklanadi va faqat o'sha qarzga ruxsati bor odamlarga ko'rinadi. "Mijoz" va "Hujjat" rasmlari faqat do'kon a'zolariga ko'rinadi, qarshi tomonga yuborilmaydi. Rasm olish uchun ilova telefonning standart kamera yoki galereya oynasini ochadi, ilova kamera yoki galereyaga doimiy ruxsat so'ramaydi. Rasmni o'chirsangiz, u serverdan ham o'chadi.
  • Nasiya va mijoz hujjatlari: do'kon rejimida nasiya (bo'lib to'lash) savdosini yozsangiz, tovarlar, to'lov grafigi va ixtiyoriy ravishda mijozning hujjat raqamlari (ID pasport, chet el pasporti, haydovchilik guvohnomasi, avtomobil raqami va texnik pasport raqami, manzil) hamda shu hujjatlarning rasmlari saqlanadi. Qaysi hujjatlar majburiyligini do'kon egasi belgilaydi. Bu ma'lumotlar faqat do'kon a'zolariga ko'rinadi, mijozga va boshqa tomonlarga yuborilmaydi, reklama yoki boshqa maqsadda ishlatilmaydi. Ilova ularni davlat organlariga (jumladan MIBga) yubormaydi. Shartnoma (PDF) namunasi telefoningizda yaratiladi va uni faqat siz o'zingiz tanlagan ilova orqali yuborasiz.
  • Zaxira nusxa: server ma'lumotlari har kuni nusxalanadi. Tashqi nusxa shifrlangan holda dasturchining shaxsiy Telegram chatiga yuboriladi. Nusxalar ma'lumotlarni tiklash uchun ishlatiladi, boshqa maqsadda emas.

03 SMS xabarlar

Qarz yoki avans qo'shayotganda telefon raqamini kiritib, "SMS yuborish"ni tanlasangiz, ilova shu raqamga sizning SIM kartangizdan bitta SMS yuboradi. SMS'da quyidagilar bo'ladi: siz kiritgan ismingiz, summa, sana, qaytarish muddati (bo'lsa) va yozuvni qabul qiluvchining Deptor ilovasiga qo'shish uchun havola.

  • SMS faqat siz tanlagan raqamga va faqat siz tasdiqlaganda yuboriladi.
  • SMS narxi mobil operatoringiz tarifiga ko'ra hisoblanadi.
  • Ilova kelgan SMS'laringizni o'qimaydi va SMS tarixiga kirmaydi.

04 Kontaktlar

Telefon raqamini kontaktlardan tanlash uchun ilova Android'ning standart kontakt tanlash oynasini ochadi. Ilovaga faqat siz tanlagan bitta kontaktning ismi va raqami beriladi. Ilova kontaktlar ro'yxatingizni o'qimaydi va saqlamaydi va buning uchun ruxsat so'ramaydi.

05 Havolalar

SMS'dagi havola (deptor.uz) qabul qiluvchining telefonida Deptor ilovasini ochadi va yozuvni qo'shishni taklif qiladi. Havolada faqat SMS matnidagi ma'lumotlar (ism, summa, sana) bor. Havola brauzerda ochilsa, sayt odatdagidek texnik so'rov ma'lumotlarini (IP manzil, brauzer turi) qayd etishi mumkin; biz ulardan foydalanuvchilarni aniqlash uchun foydalanmaymiz.

06 Ruxsatlar

  • SMS yuborish — siz tanlagan raqamga qarz/avans haqida xabar yuborish uchun.
  • Bildirishnomalar — kundalik eslatma, hafta/oy/yil hisobotlari va qarz muddati eslatmasi uchun.
  • Telefon yoqilganda ishga tushish — telefon qayta yoqilganda rejalashtirilgan eslatmalarni tiklash uchun.

Bu ruxsatlarning hech biri ma'lumot yig'ish yoki uzatish uchun ishlatilmaydi.

07 Uchinchi tomonlar, reklama va analitika

Ilovada reklama tarmoqlari, kuzatuvchi analitika yoki xatolarni yig'uvchi tashqi xizmatlar yo'q. Ma'lumotlaringiz sotilmaydi. Server ishlashi uchun Telegram (kirish) va xosting provayderi (Render) xizmatlaridan foydalaniladi.

08 Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Har qanday yozuvni ilovaning o'zida o'chirishingiz mumkin. Ilovani telefondan o'chirsangiz, telefondagi ma'lumotlar o'chadi. Serverda bog'langan qarzlar nusxasi qolgan bo'lsa, uni o'chirish uchun 11-bo'limdagi manzilga yozing.

09 Bolalar

Ilova moliyaviy yozuvlar uchun mo'ljallangan va 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalarga qaratilmagan. Biz hech kimdan shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaymiz.

10 O'zgartirishlar

Ushbu siyosat yangilanishi mumkin. O'zgarishlar shu sahifada yangi "amal qilish sanasi" bilan e'lon qilinadi.

English

Deptor is a mobile app for recording daily expenses, money you lent or borrowed, and advances/payments, with weekly, monthly and yearly reports. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

In short: your records are stored on your device. Unless you turn on the optional "Link with friends" feature (sign-in via Telegram), nothing is sent to a server. If you do, only debts that have a phone number, and their repayments, are sent so the other person can see them. No ads, no tracking analytics.

01 Data stored

  • Expenses: amount, category, note and date.
  • Debts and advances/payments: the person's name, an optional phone number, amount, date, due date and note.
  • Settings: language, currency, reminder time and the name shown in your SMS.

This data is stored in a database on your device. Expenses and income are never sent to a server. Debts are sent only if the optional linking below is on and a phone number was entered for the debt.

02 Link with friends (optional)

If you sign in with Telegram in Settings, your debt also appears in the other person's Deptor app (they confirm or reject it). To make this work:

  • You share your number with our Telegram bot; it receives your verified phone number and Telegram ID (to confirm sign-in).
  • Sent to the server: your phone number, your name as shown in SMS, debts that have a phone number (the other party's number, amount, currency, date, due date, note, your label) and their repayments.
  • The other party sees only that debt and its repayments, plus your name and number. They do not see your other records.
  • The data is stored only to run this feature, is never sold and is not used for ads.
  • When you delete a record, it is marked deleted on the server and disappears for the other party too. "Sign out" revokes your sign-in. To have all your server-side data fully erased, email abuustoz@gmail.com.
  • Communication uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Shop mode: if you create a shop and add sellers, records marked as "shop debt" are visible and editable by all shop members. Joining requires accepting an invitation. A removed member stops seeing the shop's debts.
  • Change history: the server records who did what and when on a debt (created, edited, deleted, added a repayment). The history is visible to the debt's creating side. Deleted debts are kept on the server and can be restored.
  • Photos: if you attach a photo to a debt (for example the borrower, a document or proof), it is stored on your phone. If the debt is linked with another person or a shop, the photo is also uploaded to the server and shown only to people who have access to that debt. "Customer" and "Document" photos are visible only to shop members and are not sent to the other party. To take a photo, the app opens the phone's standard camera or gallery picker and does not request ongoing camera or gallery permission. If you delete a photo, it is removed from the server as well.
  • Credit sales and customer documents: in shop mode, when you record a credit (installment) sale, the items, the payment schedule and, optionally, the customer's document details (ID card, foreign passport, driver's license, vehicle plate and registration numbers, address) and photos of those documents are stored. The shop owner decides which documents are required. This data is visible only to shop members, is not sent to the customer or other parties, and is not used for advertising or any other purpose. The app does not send it to any authority. A sample contract (PDF) is generated on your phone and you share it only through the app you choose.
  • Backups: server data is backed up daily. An off-site copy is sent, encrypted, to the developer's private Telegram chat. Backups are used only to restore data.

03 SMS messages

If you enter a phone number for a debt or advance and choose "Send SMS", the app sends one SMS to that number from your own SIM card. It contains your name as you entered it, the amount, the date, the due date (if any) and a link that lets the recipient add the record to their own Deptor app.

  • SMS is sent only to the number you choose and only when you confirm.
  • Your mobile operator charges for the SMS according to your plan.
  • The app does not read your incoming messages or SMS history.

04 Contacts

To pick a phone number, the app opens Android's standard contact picker. Only the name and number of the single contact you select are passed to the app. The app does not read or store your contact list and does not request contacts permission.

05 Links

The link in the SMS (deptor.uz) opens Deptor on the recipient's phone and offers to add the record. It contains only the information already in the SMS text (name, amount, date). If the link is opened in a browser, the website may log standard technical request data (IP address, browser type); we do not use it to identify users.

06 Permissions

  • Send SMS — to send a debt/advance message to the number you choose.
  • Notifications — daily reminder, week/month/year reports and due-date reminders.
  • Run at startup — to restore scheduled reminders after a reboot.

None of these permissions is used to collect or transmit data.

07 Third parties, ads and analytics

The app contains no advertising networks, tracking analytics or external crash-reporting services. Your data is never sold. The linking feature relies on Telegram (sign-in) and a hosting provider (Render).

08 Deleting data

You can delete any record inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone. If linked debts remain on the server, email the address in section 11 to have them erased.

09 Children

The app is meant for financial record keeping and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone.
