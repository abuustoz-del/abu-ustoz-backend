Deptor

Hisobni va ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Qanday so'rash mumkin: abuustoz@gmail.com manziliga "Deptor hisobini o'chiring" mavzusida xat yuboring va Deptor'ga kirgan telefon raqamingizni yozing. Egasi ekaningizni tasdiqlash uchun sizdan qo'shimcha savol so'ralishi mumkin.

So'rov 30 kun ichida bajariladi.

Nima o'chiriladi

  • Hisobingiz va telefon raqamingiz bilan bog'lanish;
  • serverga yuborilgan qarzlar, to'lovlar, nasiya savdolari va mijoz hujjatlari;
  • serverdagi rasmlar;
  • do'kon a'zoligi (siz do'kon egasi bo'lsangiz, do'kon ma'lumotlari ham).

Nima saqlanib qoladi

  • Server zaxira nusxalari ma'lumotlarni tiklash uchun saqlanadi va 14 kun ichida avtomatik almashadi, shundan keyin ma'lumotingiz zaxiralardan ham yo'qoladi;
  • boshqa foydalanuvchining telefonida allaqachon turgan nusxalar (masalan, sizning qarzingiz ko'rsatilgan yozuv) o'sha odamning o'zida qoladi.

Telefondagi ma'lumotlar

Ilova telefonda saqlagan yozuvlar (xarajatlar, kirimlar, qarzlar) faqat sizning qurilmangizda. Ularni o'chirish uchun ilovani o'chiring (Sozlamalar → Ilovalar → Deptor → O'chirish) yoki ilova ma'lumotlarini tozalang.

Delete your account and data

How to request: email abuustoz@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Deptor account" and include the phone number you use in Deptor. We may ask a verification question to confirm ownership.

Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its link to your phone number;
  • debts, payments, credit sales and customer documents sent to the server;
  • photos stored on the server;
  • shop membership (if you own a shop, the shop's data too).

What is kept

  • Server backups are kept only for recovery and are replaced automatically within 14 days, after which your data disappears from backups too;
  • copies already on another user's phone (for example a record that mentions you) stay with that person.

Data on your phone

Records the app keeps on your phone (expenses, income, debts) exist only on your device. Uninstall the app or clear its data to remove them.
